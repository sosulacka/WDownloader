# Privacy policy

Revision of 28 November 2026. This policy describes the Wdownloader desktop app. GitHub project pages and third-party platforms have their own policies.

## Data on your device

Wdownloader saves your settings, download folder, language, appearance and document acceptance locally. Download history contains links, titles, settings, status and output paths. Previews can create a temporary media copy and waveform. The app does not send history to the developer and includes no built-in analytics or advertising trackers.

If you connect your own YouTube session, the app stores YouTube cookies in its local data folder so that yt-dlp can make requests from your account. Temporary copies are deleted after normal completion. Session files can provide access to your account. Keep them private and leave them out of error reports.

## Network requests

When you open a link, Wdownloader and yt-dlp contact the platform and its media and image servers. They receive your IP address, request parameters and information needed to access the selected material. The Google sign-in page processes login data under Google's rules. These requests do not pass through a developer-operated server.

Wdownloader fetches tool updates and components from GitHub and the providers' repositories. Enabling SponsorBlock sends requests to sponsor.ajay.app. To display segments, Wdownloader sends the first four characters of the hashed video ID. The service receives the request's IP address. When you save with SponsorBlock enabled, yt-dlp also contacts the service.

## Deleting data

Disconnect the YouTube session in Settings and clear history on the History page. When uninstalling Wdownloader, you can remove its settings, history, cache and tools. Downloaded files and incomplete downloads stay in your chosen folder. The documents are available in Settings. Acceptance is stored on your device.
